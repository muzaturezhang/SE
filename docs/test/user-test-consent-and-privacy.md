# User Test — Consent and Privacy Script

Status: template only. A completed consent record must not be fabricated.

## Participant information

This study evaluates a coursework prototype of a Holiday Resort Booking System. We want to learn whether the screens and flows are understandable. This is not a test of your intelligence or hotel knowledge.

- Participation is voluntary.
- You may stop at any time without giving a reason.
- The prototype does not process real payments.
- Use fictional information during the session.
- The observer will record task completion, time, errors and general comments.
- Audio/video will be recorded only if you explicitly agree.
- Your name and contact details will not appear in the report.
- We will use a participant code such as `P-C-01` or `P-S-01`.
- Screenshots will be cropped or redacted to remove personal information.
- Evidence will be retained only for the agreed coursework period and accessible only to the project team.
- You may request deletion of your recording before the final evidence package is submitted.
